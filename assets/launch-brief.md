# Vertical OS launch brief

Complete the destination and intended delivery before sending this brief to a new chat. Use human-readable environment and workspace names; the receiving agent resolves their identities. Supply a segment and geography for focused research. The source app and destination app are separate.

```text
Use $vertical-os-builder to create Insurance OS.
Industry scope: US property & casualty insurance.
Target environment/tenant: <destination environment or tenant name>.
Target workspace: <destination workspace name>.
App: Insurance OS (new slug: insurance-os).
Delivery: <local build/preview OR create, publish and activate in the named target>.

Clone the existing Banking/Retail source. Preserve sphere behavior, alignment and shared interactions; reuse suitable flows. Complete the in-depth research workbook and adapt the departments, agents and journey architectures. Keep real demo journey implementation as separate tasks.
Include Tower (Agents, Journeys and Org) and Retail's company-wide Work overview in the default navigation; omit standalone Brief and Controls tabs/pages unless explicitly requested. Work should show department pulse cards, a company-wide activity list, combined search/department/status filters and viewer scope without requiring flow drill-downs. Preserve view context and label illustrative activity as a Demo snapshot, separate from implementation readiness. Deliver the research workbook separately and retain relevant policy/ownership information in existing details.
Allocate agents by business importance and distinct workflow coverage, not equal quotas per department. Core business gets a clear majority; primary core departments should have at least twice the agents of individual support departments by default. HR must be smaller than every core department. Document the classification, counts and rationale, consolidate support capabilities into skills, and let the inherited ring reflect the real size differences.
Render complete names with no clipped letters. Keep all agent logos bright and colorful across views and filters, including Planned roles. Preserve Retail's original Tower connector styling through its full CSS cascade: thin accent-colored curves, subtle glow and matching dots, with no thick body-ink overrides. On the Agents page, show connection lines and slow traveling dots only when an agent is hovered or clicked/selected; keep the page calm at rest. Preserve truthful status, reduced-motion support and hidden-view suspension.
Add a Light/Dark control in the top right, default to Light, and remember the choice across reloads. Theme every app view and detail surface consistently, keep the setting scoped to the app, and preserve complete names, colorful spheres and calm connections in both modes.
```

The insurance segment above is an example choice; change it when life, health, commercial lines, brokerage or another scope is intended. For a local-only test, set the target to local-only and delivery to local build/preview. New chats on the same Wonderful Code profile can invoke the global skill directly. On another profile/machine, provide the complete skill folder and access to the original app source.
